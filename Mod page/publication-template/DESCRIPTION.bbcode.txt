[h1]Floppy Fabrics[/h1]
Have you ever noticed how unnatural fabric looks in the wind? Even in a heavy storm, with gusts tearing down the street, your curtains and the flags outside hang as if they were carved from stone.

With this mod, wind is no longer something the game only tells you about. Now you can actually see it, and the world feels far more alive for it.

Real, dynamic physics for every fabric in Project Zomboid. Flags, curtains, banners, bunting and awnings billow in the wind, swing when something brushes past, and react to players, zombies and vehicles.

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[b]Build 42.21+ only.[/b]
[b]ZombieBuddy is required[/b] (this is a Java mod): subscribe to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url], install its launcher hook (see its page), then enable [b]ZombieBuddy[/b] and [b]Floppy Fabrics[/b] in the mod manager (ZombieBuddy version 2.3.4 or newer).

On first launch ZombieBuddy asks you to approve the mod's Java jar.
The jar is unsigned for now, so ZombieBuddy asks again after each update. Signing returns once the author is in ZombieBuddy's known authors.

The mod is visual only and does not touch your saves, but approve Java code only from authors you trust.

[h1]What moves[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Flags:[/b] wall flags, the American flag on its pole (turns with the wind, droops when calm), the Fort Knox flags and the white bar banner. Multi-tile flags are one cloth across their tiles, with no seams.
[*][b]Curtains and sheets:[/b] window curtains and player-hung sheets (they move while the window is open or broken; sheets on doors move while they face the outdoors, and any sheet hung on the outside of a window moves whatever the window is doing), hospital privacy curtains, theatre stage curtains and car-wash entrance covers (they hang free across the middle of their square and can be pushed from either side).
[*][b]Banners and cloths:[/b] church banners, the Riverside street banner (16 tiles, hung from a sagging cable) and the banners on its light poles, altar cloths.
[*][b]Strings, awnings and canopies:[/b] bunting and string lights swing on their strings (the glow of lit bulbs follows them); awnings and market canopies billow and flap in strong wind while their valances flutter.
[*][b]Tents and shelters:[/b] the tarp tent, all four camping tents, the improvised tent, and the tarp and hide shelters breathe in the wind and flap in a storm. The military tents (green and tan) move too: roof, gables and eaves. Poles, pegs, floors and walls stay put.
[*][b]Nets:[/b] volleyball nets and the military training nets (the cargo net under a beam and the tall net between posts) sway and give when you walk into them, each as one piece across its whole length. Poles and frames stay put.
[*][b]Fence and doorway covers:[/b] sheets and tarps from [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3785740658]Fence Sheets[/url] (see Compatible mods).
[/list]
Every supported tile, with a preview picture, is listed in the [url=https://github.com/Svi-ra/floppy-fabrics-mod/blob/main/docs/TILE_REGISTRY.md]tile registry[/url].

[h1]How it behaves[/h1]
[list]
[*]Follows the game's own wind (speed and direction). Wind indoors is zero, so nothing moves in a closed room.
[*]Players, zombies and vehicles push the cloth and drag it by friction. Walls and furniture stop it.
[*]Curtains move only while their window is open, smashed or an empty frame. A closed curtain lifted by wind lets a little daylight flicker into the room.
[*]With no wind, fabric is pixel-identical to vanilla.
[*][b]Sound:[/b] cloth flaps, rustles and snaps as it moves, and fades as you zoom out. Zombies do not hear it.
[*][b]Gameplay is untouched.[/b] Movement is drawing only. When curtains, sheets or tarps move in the wind, nothing about what zombies can see changes: a hung sheet blocks their line of sight exactly as it does in vanilla, open or closed, still or swaying. The mod does not touch vision, noise, collision or any game rule.
[/list]

[h1]Settings[/h1]
[b]Options > Mods > Floppy Fabrics[/b]: an Enable switch; Wind (Vanilla, Calm, Breezy, Windy or Custom, felt only by fabric); Quality (Low to Ultra, or Custom); sound volume; switches for interactions, curtains, curtain light and decorations.

[h1]Performance[/h1]
[list]
[*]Only cloth that is awake (wind or contact) is simulated. Cloth with no wind and nothing touching it is drawn exactly like vanilla and costs nothing extra.
[*]Measured on the author's PC at default quality: the mod's CPU time stayed under 1 ms per frame with about 50 flags awake at once. At low quality with 30 flags it was about 0.3 ms per frame.
[*]Cost grows with how many cloths move at once: a storm over a town of flags costs more than a quiet room.
[*]Single frames can briefly reach about 2 ms. Not yet tested on low-end PCs, very large cities or dedicated servers.
[*]If you see a frame-rate drop, set Quality to Low first. Report it with your PC specs.
[/list]

[h1]Compatible mods[/h1]
[b]Wind sway support:[/b] Floppy Fabrics animates the fabric these mods add, so their sheets and tarps sway in the wind with no extra setup. You do not need any of them, and nothing changes if you do not use them.
[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3785740658][img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/11810053326347896396/BAC2DE2B8B6F5579A4F5F0C02DC1D365FFE78DA5/?imw=128&imh=128[/img][/url]
[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3785740658][b]Fence Sheets[/b][/url]: sheets and tarps hung on fences and empty door frames billow in the wind and get pushed aside when something walks through. Tarps stay tied at the top and sides, so a run of tarp tiles moves as one sheet.

[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3794207681][img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/11949640646152024147/951BB1502A99B420FCCDB378A7B50001792AD437/?imw=128&imh=128[/img][/url]
[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3794207681][b]Fitted Sheets[/b][/url]: made-to-measure sheets on windows move like the vanilla ones, including on the outside of a window.

[h1]Safe to add and remove[/h1]
Visual only and client-side. It adds no new objects, no mod data and nothing to your save. Disable the mod and everything returns to vanilla.
[b]Multiplayer:[/b] nothing needs to be installed on the server and nothing is synced, because wind visuals are client-side. This has not been tested on a dedicated server yet.

[h1]Known issues[/h1]
[list]
[*]Much of version 0.6.0 (flutter, multi-tile cloths, street and pole banners, strings, canopies, altar cloths) has not yet been played through in the game. Expect rough edges and please report them.
[*][*]No Viewpoint support.
[*]Curtains on player-built (thumpable) windows never move.
[*]A player-hung sheet changes look when its window opens (it switches from its 3D model to its 2D sprite).
[*]Where the vanilla art of two neighbouring tiles does not line up (the Knox flag differs by about 5 px), a small step stays visible.
[*]Animals and loose items on the floor do not interact with cloth.
[/list]
